== Run an OpenSCAP scan on a host


.Run an OpenSCAP scan on a host
[%collapsible]
=====
Navigate to ** Hosts > All Hosts** and click on `rhel1.lab`.

.Hosts menu
image::scan-select-rhel1.png[]

Next, click on the `Schedule a job` button and select `Run OpenSCAP scan`.

.Run OpenSCAP scan
image::scan-run-openscap-scan.png[]

=====

.Check the status of the job
[%collapsible]
=====

To check the status of the ansible job, click on **Monitor > Jobs**.

.Jobs menu
image::monitor-jobs.png[]

Click on `Run ansible roles`.

.Run ansible roles
image::run-scan-openscap.png[]

Click on rhe accordion button next to `rhel1` to view the details of the job.

.Job details
image::run-scan-openscap-status.png[]

=====

.View the compliance report
[%collapsible]
=====

To view the compliance report, click on **Hosts > Compliance > Reports**.

.Compliance reports
image::menu-compliance-reports.png[]

Click on the `Reported At` field.

.Reported At field
image::compliance-report.png[]

You'll see the compliance report for the host.

.Compliance report
image::compliance-report-rhel1.png[]

The tailoring file used to customize the OpenSCAP scan specifies that the host should not permit empty passwords. This report shows that the host has a compliance issue with this rule. As well, you can see that the scan only looks at this one rule. If we did not apply a tailoring file, the scan would look at all the rules in the benchmark.

=====

.Remediate the compliance issues
[%collapsible]
=====

To remediate the compliance issues, in the `Actions` column, click on the `Hosts failing this rule` button and select `Remediation`.

.Remediate
image::remediate-compliance.png[]

In the `Disable SSH Access vis Empty Passwords` form, do the following:

. Check that `Remote job` is selected.
. In the `Snippet` drop down, select `Ansible`.
. Click next.

.Disable SSH Access vis Empty Passwords
image::disable-ssh-select-snippet.png[]

Click through to the `Review remediation` menu, review the changes and click on the `Run` button.

.Review remediation
image::review-remediation.png[]

On the next menu, click on the `Job details` button.

.Job details
image::job-details.png[]

You can review the details of the job and the output of the job.

.Job details
image::remediation-details.png[]

=====

The lab is now complete.

Optionally, you can run another OpenSCAP scan to verify that the compliance issues have been remediated.
