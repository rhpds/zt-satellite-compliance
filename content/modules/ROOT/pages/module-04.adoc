= Create an activation key to provide access to the required content view

In this module, you will create an activation key to provide access to the content view that includes the required repositories for OpenSCAP compliance scanning completed in the previous module.

.Create an activation key
[%collapsible]
====
Navigate to **Content > Lifecycle > Activation Keys** in the Satellite Web UI and click **Create Activation Key**.

.Activation key menu
image::activation-keys-menu.png[]

In the `New Activation Key` form, enter the following information:

. Enter the name `RHEL10` in the **Name** field.
. Click on `Assign content view environments`.

.Activation key form
image::new-activation-key.png[]

In the `Assign content view environments` form, select the following:

. Select `RHEL10` in the **Content view** field.
. Click `Save`

.Assign content view environments form
image::assign-cv.png[]

Click **Save** to create the activation key.

====
